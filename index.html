About

I've always been curious about technology since I was young. started with an old, bulky shape that ran Windows XP (when I was 7 years old); I used Google mainly for researching random stuff like how to become a vampire (such a nerd lol). Then I started learning basic HTML on the internet because I wanted to become a hacker so bad.

The internet is my best friend. It taught me to become a self-learner (I'm an undergraduate). I find it fun to make things without worrying about how the outcome will be. It feels more like playing a puzzle game than doing projects. You keep building things piece by piece until it becomes fully functional.

In the end, I'm still learning, still building, and probably still googling random stuff.
